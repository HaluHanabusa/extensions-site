If an extension is discontinued

These are small tools and some of them will not find an audience. If we stop working on one, this is exactly what happens, in this order:

  1. We publish a final update that unlocks the paid features for everyone and stops checking licence keys altogether. After that update, your copy needs nothing from us — no licence server, no account, no internet connection. This is the step that makes "pay once, keep it forever" true even after we are gone.
  2. We announce it on this site.
  3. Anyone who bought the paid upgrade within the previous 14 days is refunded automatically, without having to ask — the same window as any other refund.
  4. Only then do we remove the store listing. We never remotely disable anything you already installed.

What you lose when an extension is discontinued is future updates, not the software you paid for. That is why the automatic refund covers recent buyers rather than everyone: someone who bought last week did not get what they expected, while someone who has been using it for a year did.
